# Memo on the Flamingo Revolution

*Last updated: 29 June 2026*

## The unProtected Area

The Vjosa–Narta region is one of Albania's most valuable coastal landscapes and one of its most important biodiversity hotspots. Its lagoons, wetlands, coastal forests, marine habitats and protected areas form a unique ecological corridor along the Adriatic coast.

- **42 km²** of lagoon and wetland habitat
- **194 km²** of protected landscape spanning river delta, forest and coast
- **200+ bird species** recorded
- Thousands of flamingos observed annually
- One of Albania's most important wetlands for migratory birds
- Part of the Adriatic Flyway between Europe and Africa

Since late April 2026, construction activities have taken place in the Zvërnec area. Residents, environmental organizations and civil-society groups say there has been insufficient public information about the project's scope, environmental studies, legal basis and potential impacts.

### Principal risks

| Risk | Severity | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disturbance of migratory routes, habitat degradation and reduced breeding and wintering success | Very high | Narta Lagoon ecosystem and Adriatic Flyway |
| Permanent loss and fragmentation of wetlands, coastal forests and wildlife habitats | Very high | Local and regional |
| Roads, utilities, marinas and services increasing pollution, urbanization and disturbance | High | Landscape-wide |
| Privatization of accessible coastal landscapes and restrictions on traditional public uses | High | Coast, beaches and surrounding public spaces |

## The Flamingo Revolution

Peaceful protests have continued across Albania since early May in response to the construction. Local opposition became a broad environmental and civic movement advocating for environmental protection, transparency, the rule of law and democratic accountability.

The movement includes environmental organizations such as PPNEA, EcoAlbania, EuroNatur, BirdLife International and Riverwatch; Albanian and international scientists; local communities; civil-society and youth groups; artists; public figures; and Albanian diaspora communities across Europe and North America.

### Movement overview

- More than 30 consecutive days of coordinated peaceful protests across Albania
- Solidarity demonstrations in more than 30 Albanian diaspora cities
- Tens of thousands mobilized through demonstrations, marches and public events
- Multiple legal complaints and court actions by environmental and civil-society organizations

### What protesters demand

1. The resignation of the Government of Albania.
2. Repeal of the 2024 amendments to the Law on Protected Areas.
3. Repeal of the Strategic Investors legal framework.
4. Repeal of the Mountains Package (*Paketa e Maleve*).
5. Repeal of the amendments to the Law on Cultural Heritage.

On 17 June 2026, the European Parliament called for the repeal of the 2024 amendments to the Law on Protected Areas and an immediate moratorium on new construction within protected areas.

## Timeline

| Year | Key development |
| --- | --- |
| 2015 | Law No. 55/2015 on Strategic Investments introduces fast-track procedures and special legal status. |
| 2021 | Construction of Vlora International Airport begins inside the Vjosa–Narta Protected Landscape; conservation groups and scientists raise concerns and pursue legal action. |
| 2023 | Airport construction continues. Albania creates the Vjosa Wild River National Park while warnings continue about cumulative impacts on Vjosa–Narta. |
| 2024 | Large tourism developments are announced for Sazan and Zvërnec. Parliament changes the Law on Protected Areas, permitting some tourism and strategic-investment activity inside protected areas. |
| 2025 | The Sazan development receives Strategic Investor status. Parliament adopts the “Mountain Package”; legal challenges and public campaigns intensify. |
| 2026 | Construction begins in Zvërnec, prompting protests in Albania and more than 30 diaspora cities. On 17 June, the European Parliament calls for restored protections and a construction moratorium. |
