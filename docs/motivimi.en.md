# Motivation

Flamingo Berlin began in response to developments threatening nature, the public interest and transparency in Albania. The protests are not merely opposition to one project; they are a broader call to uphold the law, protect designated areas and ensure open, accountable decision-making.

---

## What does the memo contain?

The **Flamingo Revolution** memo describes the movement and documents the main facts behind the protests. It covers:

- 🌿 the ecological importance of Vjosa–Narta;
- 📜 the history of key developments and decisions;
- 📅 a chronology of the protests;
- 📢 the protesters' demands;
- 📚 supporting documents, sources and references.
