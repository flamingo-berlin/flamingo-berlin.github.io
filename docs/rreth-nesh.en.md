# About Flamingo Berlin

**Flamingo Berlin** is a civic initiative of the Albanian diaspora in Berlin. We organize peaceful protests and actions, share information and create opportunities for civic cooperation in defence of nature, democracy and the public interest in Albania.

---

## What we do

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-bullhorn-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Protest** — We organize protests and public activities every Sunday.
- :material-newspaper-variant-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Inform** — We share facts and developments about the issues we follow.
- :material-folder-open-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Prepare** — We create open resources, documents and materials for everyone.

</div>

---

## Our principles

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-leaf:{ .lg .middle } **Peaceful** — We support peaceful, democratic action only.
- :material-hand-heart-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Open** — Everyone can contribute according to their time, skills and means.
- :material-scale-balance:{ .lg .middle } **Independent** — Our work is not directed by party-political or financial interests.
- :material-book-open-variant-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Verifiable** — Our information is based on documents and sources that can be checked.
- :material-chat-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Respectful** — We discuss ideas respectfully and do not tolerate personal attacks or discrimination.

</div>

---

## Partners and our network

We work together with:

- **FlamingoRevolution.eu**
- **Diaspora Zbarkon**
- **Admins of Diaspora Protests**

We are also in contact with **PPNEA**, **EuroNatur** and other organizations working to protect nature and the public interest.

---

## Join us

Flamingo Berlin is open to anyone who wants to contribute peacefully and constructively. You do not need to be an organizer. You can help with events, research, communications, design, photography, translation, logistics or institutional outreach.
